This is a high effort action.
Understand a problem deeply, think about core decisions, and understand clearly the best path forward.

Create a brief document with your findings, and curate it as we continue the session.
Maintain it clean and pure as we find new information or we take decisions.
It's the clean fundamental representation of the topic and the state of work.

**Scope** is the argument. Without one, brief this session's work plus uncommitted changes.

## Brief

The format is:

**Bottom line up front**

# Summary

# Options and Tradeoffs

# Path forward (and why)

# References (for agents)

---

It's short and to the point. No narrative. The core clean information that someone needs to understand the problem and decisions.
It's pure of secondary details. A brief is the base to generate other more detailed artifacts for coordination.

Excluding references, the brief should fit in a single screen.
