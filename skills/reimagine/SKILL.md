Do some deep rethinking of [topic] in this codebase.

Break compatibility, and rebuild solutions from first principles as if building today.
Consider best practices, research good sources, and evaluate creative alternatives.
Can we change it fundamentally in a way the helps the long term and brings a strong or unexpected win?

Integrate proposals for new solution as a well-engineered section to our architecture.
The report should be clean of noise and focus on the essentials to understand the proposals with implications and tradeoffs.

When relevant, call the Skill "codebase-design" for design vocabulary.
